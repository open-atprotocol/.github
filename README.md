# open-atprotocol

## What is this?

A set of free and Open Source implementations of the AT protocol and clients
independent of Bluesky PBC

## Why is this?

I love Bluesky and I love AT protocol. I love its inherent freedom. I do not
love wading through skeets of chuds stating with their whole chests that this
is definitively just like X/Twitter because *hand waving ensues*.

You can call this adversarial collaboration. Call it a safety net. I don't care.
This is largely a learning experience for me, Josh Ghiloni ([github](https://github.com/jghiloni) | [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/profile/did:plc:e2fun4xcfwtcrqfdwhfnghxk)).

There will be implementations of both the core components of AT protocol (PDS, MST, etc)
in various languages by various contributors, and they will all share some common
features:

1. They will be free and open-source forever.
1. They will foster an open sense of community.
1. When they are in a language supported by Bluesky PBC, they will not use any
   implementations from Bluesky PBC.

## Why that last one, though? You think you're better than them?

Hell no! The team at Bluesky PBC is made up of some of the smartest folks in the
world. The point of this effort is to prove to the naysayers that Bluesky needs
AT Protocol a lot more than AT Protocol needs Bluesky, and it can thrive outside of
that ecosystem.

## How can I get in on this?

If you want to contribute a client or server implementation, that's coming soon.
We'll have a CLA for you to sign that ensures that you're willing to stick to the
three items above. If you're interested, DM me on Bluesky (see above).
